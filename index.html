FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

What does CWO Strategy Group do?

CWO Strategy Group is a small technology firm based in Dayton and Columbus, Ohio. We do two things: we install private AI systems that run on hardware inside your business, and we build, manage and optimize websites so customers find you on Google and in AI answers like ChatGPT and Perplexity.

What is a private AI system?

A private AI system is AI that runs on hardware you own, inside your office, instead of on a cloud service. Your documents and financial data stay in your building, there are no per-seat subscriptions, and it keeps working without an internet connection.

How much does website management cost?

Website management starts at $28 a month and scales with the size of the site and how often it changes. New websites and private AI systems are quoted in writing, at a fixed price, after a free 30-minute call.

Where are you located?

We're based in Dayton and Columbus, Ohio, and meet clients in person in both areas. We work with businesses in all 50 states by phone and video.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Website management is month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Every project is scoped and priced in writing before work begins.

Is private AI the same as using ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT and similar tools run in a provider's data center, so your prompts and files travel there. Our systems run open-weight AI models on hardware in your building, set up for your workflows and connected to your own files and software.
