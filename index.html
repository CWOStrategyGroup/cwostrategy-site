FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

What does CWO Strategy Group do?

We do three things: build custom hand-coded websites, manage and update them after launch, and run SEO so the site gets found. One team, one point of contact, no account managers in between.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing scales to the needs of the business rather than being sold as one fixed package. A simple brochure site for a local shop costs far less than a multi-section site with ongoing SEO. Every quote is given up front in writing after a free 30-minute call.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small business websites go from kickoff to launch in two to five days. The main variable is how quickly you return content, photos, and approvals — not the build itself.

Do you use WordPress or website builders?

No. Every site is coded from scratch. That avoids plugin bloat, improves load speed, and gives full control over the markup that search engines and AI systems read.

Who owns the website after launch?

You do, however, we handle the domain, site, and other online services for you so its a full hands off experience.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and no firm honestly can. Search engines control their own ranking systems. We commit to sound technical implementation, white-hat practices, and transparent reporting instead.

Do you work with businesses outside Ohio?

Yes. We're based in Columbus and work with clients in all 50 states over Zoom and phone. Local clients can meet in person; everyone else gets the same process remotely.

What happens after my website launches?

You can view the site and request changes at any time. Managed clients get content updates, fixes, uptime monitoring, and a free portal login showing live traffic and keyword positions.
