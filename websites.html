Search in 2026

What are SEO, AEO and GEO?

People now find businesses three ways: search results, direct answers, and AI recommendations. We optimize for all three, because a customer asking ChatGPT for “the best bakery near me” never sees page two of Google.

SEO

Search engine optimization

Ranking on Google and Bing through speed, clean structure, local signals and content that matches what people search.

AEO

Answer engine optimization

Structuring your pages with clear questions, direct answers and schema so assistants and featured snippets can quote you.

GEO

Generative engine optimization

Making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews to understand, trust and cite.

FAQ

Website questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

Website management starts at $28 a month, depending on the size of the site and how often it changes. New builds and redesigns are quoted at a fixed price, in writing, after a free 30-minute call, and the price doesn't change unless you approve new work.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most small-business websites take two to five weeks from approved scope to launch. The biggest variable is how quickly we receive your content and approvals, not the build itself.

Do you use WordPress or website builders?

No. Every site is designed and hand-coded from scratch, with no themes, page builders or plugin sprawl. That's what keeps our sites fast, secure and easy to manage.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO (search engine optimization) helps your site rank on Google and Bing. AEO (answer engine optimization) structures your content so assistants and featured snippets can quote it directly. GEO (generative engine optimization) makes your business more likely to be cited and recommended by AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. We commit to the technical work, the content and the structured data, and we show you every month what changed and what it did.

Who owns the website?

You do. On final payment, the custom code, design and content we create transfer to you. You also hold the rights to your domain. To keep things hands-off, we purchase and manage it for you, along with hosting, updates and security, so nothing breaks or expires. If you ever leave, we transfer it to you.

Do I need to buy my own domain?

No. We purchase your domain and manage it for you: registration, renewals and DNS settings are all handled, so you never have to log in to a registrar. The rights to the domain are yours, and we'll transfer it to you if you ever leave.

Can I cancel website management?

Yes. Management is month to month and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. There are no long-term contracts.

Do you work with businesses outside Ohio?

Yes. We meet clients in person around Dayton and Columbus and work with businesses in all 50 states by phone and video.
