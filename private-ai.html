What it is

What is a private AI system?

A private AI system is AI that runs on hardware you own, inside your office, instead of on a cloud service. Your documents and financial data stay in your building, there are no per-seat subscriptions, and it keeps working without an internet connection.

  • Private by designPrompts, files and answers are processed on your own hardware.
  • Yours to keepYou own the machine. No per-seat pricing that grows with your team.
  • Built around your workConnected to your folders, accounting exports and the tasks you repeat.

Private vs. cloud

Why run AI locally instead of using ChatGPT?

Run AI locally when privacy, predictable cost and working with your own data matter more than raw general-purpose ability. Cloud AI is still the stronger choice for open-ended tasks, and we'll tell you if that's what you need.

FAQ

Private AI questions, answered.

Does my data ever leave my building?

Not for the AI work itself. Your documents, prompts and answers are processed on hardware in your office, on your network, and the system can run fully offline. If you want us to manage it remotely, that access is set up with your approval and can be switched off.

Can a private AI system do our accounting?

It can take a large share of the repetitive work off your team: reading invoices and receipts, drafting entries, categorizing transactions, flagging items that don't reconcile, and preparing reports. A person on your team reviews and approves the work before anything is final. It supports your bookkeeper or accountant rather than replacing them, and CWO Strategy Group is not a CPA firm.

What hardware do we get?

It depends on your team size and workload, from a single desktop AI workstation to a multi-GPU server or a rack-mounted system. We size it after an assessment, source it, install it, and you own it.

Which AI models do you use?

Open-weight models, meaning AI models that can be licensed to run on your own hardware, chosen for each job. We test them on your real tasks before rollout and upgrade them as better models are released.

How does private AI compare with ChatGPT or other cloud AI?

The largest cloud models are still stronger at open-ended, general-purpose tasks. Private AI makes more sense when privacy, predictable costs, offline operation and working directly with your own data matter more, which describes much of day-to-day business work. We'll tell you honestly which one fits.

How much does a private AI system cost?

Every system is quoted in writing after an assessment: hardware, installation and setup as a one-time cost, plus a flat monthly management plan. There are no per-seat fees. Founding clients receive founding pricing.

Do we need our own IT department?

No. We install the system, connect it to your files and software, train your team, and manage it afterwards. Your team uses it through a simple chat-style interface in a web browser.

Have you installed private AI for other businesses before?

Private AI is our newest service, and we're upfront about that. We've built and tested our setups on laptop-class hardware; client systems use the same approach on dedicated hardware sized for the job. That's why we're starting with a founding client program: a small number of businesses get founding pricing and direct access to the team building their system.

CWO Strategy Group is not a CPA firm and does not provide accounting, tax, legal or audit services. Our systems support your team and your professional advisors. We design systems so sensitive data stays on your premises; we don't certify regulatory compliance (such as HIPAA) and recommend involving your compliance advisor.
