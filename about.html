Operating principles

The rules we don't break.

01

The price is the price.

Scope and cost are agreed in writing before work begins. Extra work is quoted and approved first, never billed afterwards.

02

Hands-off, but yours.

We purchase and manage your domain, hosting and systems so nothing quietly breaks or expires. You keep the rights to all of it.

03

Your data stays yours.

No third-party trackers on our sites. Private AI that runs on your hardware, in your building.

04

No guarantees we can't keep.

We don't promise first-page rankings or AI that replaces people. We commit to the work and show you what changed.

05

We'll say no.

If a project is outside what we do well, or something we don't believe in, we'll tell you and point you somewhere better.

06

Same-day replies.

You email a person, not a queue. Most replies land the same day; one business day at most.
