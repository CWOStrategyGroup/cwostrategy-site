1. Who we are

CWO Strategy Group, LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”) builds and manages private AI systems and websites, with team members in Dayton and Columbus, Ohio. This policy explains how we handle information collected through cwostrategy.com, our Private AI services, and our direct business communications.

Data controller: CWO Strategy Group, LLC, Ohio, United States. Contact: cwostrategygroup@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Start a Project form: name, email, phone (optional), business name, current website (optional), budget range (optional), the services you're interested in, and your message.
  • Email and phone: whatever you choose to send us, plus your address or number.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: IP address, browser user agent, page requested and timestamp, used for security, rate limiting and abuse prevention. IP addresses from our forms are stored only as salted hashes.
  • No cookies: this site sets none.
  • Aggregate analytics: cookieless page views and referring domains through Cloudflare Web Analytics. See section 4.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect payment card numbers on this site.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, ad pixels or cross-site profiling.
  • We do not buy contact lists or enrich your record with data from brokers.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To alert our team that a new enquiry has arrived (see section 6).
  • To deliver and support the services you've engaged us for.
  • To keep the site secure: rate limiting, bot filtering and abuse investigation.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use your data to train AI models.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies at all: no advertising, analytics or session cookies.

Traffic analytics come from Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless by design. It records page views and referring domains in aggregate, without cross-site identifiers, device fingerprinting or a profile of any individual visitor.

5. Private AI systems

When we install a CWO Vault system, the documents, prompts and answers it processes stay on hardware at your premises.

  • We do not copy data processed by your system to our own systems, and we never use it to train AI models.
  • During installation, testing and support we may need to see data on the system. We do so only with your permission and only as far as the task requires.
  • Remote management access is set up only with your approval, and you can switch it off.
  • Any further data-handling commitments are set out in your written agreement, which controls if it differs from this section.

6. Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the business, and only the minimum each one needs:

  • Our web host and Cloudflare: serve the website, DNS, security and cookieless analytics.
  • Supabase: database and form processing. Hosted in Canada (ca-central-1).
  • ntfy.sh: a push notification service that relays a short alert about each new enquiry (your name, business, contact details, services and the start of your message) to our team's phones. Messages are held only briefly for delivery.
  • Google (Gmail): our business email, used to reply to you.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others. If the business is ever sold or merged, client data may transfer as part of that transaction; you would be told in advance.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't become projects: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records and correspondence: for the engagement plus 7 years, to meet tax and accounting requirements.
  • Server logs: 90 days.

8. How we protect it

  • HTTPS everywhere, with HSTS and TLS through Cloudflare.
  • Enquiries are stored in a database table that only our server can read; the public website can add to it but never read from it.
  • Rate limiting, honeypot fields and timing checks on every public form.
  • Form IP addresses stored only as salted hashes, never in raw form.
  • Data encrypted in transit and at rest by our database provider.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities without undue delay.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. Email cwostrategygroup@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. We won't charge you or treat you differently for asking.

EU and UK (GDPR): our lawful bases are consent (enquiries), contract (client work), legal obligation (tax records) and legitimate interest (security). You may complain to your supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.

10. Children's privacy

This is a business-to-business site and is not directed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us data, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page. For material changes affecting existing clients, we'll email you as well.
