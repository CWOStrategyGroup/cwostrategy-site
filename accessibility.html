Accessibility Statement

A site nobody can use isn't finished. We build to WCAG 2.1 Level AA, on our own site and on every client site we ship.

1. Conformance target

cwostrategy.com aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. We consider the site substantially conformant, with the known limitations listed below.

2. What we've implemented

  • Keyboard navigation: every link, button and form field can be reached and used by keyboard. The mobile menu moves focus into the menu when opened, closes with Esc, and returns focus to the menu button.
  • Visible focus: a clear blue outline on every focusable element.
  • Skip link: “Skip to content” is the first focusable item on every page.
  • Semantic structure: one main heading per page, headings in order, real landmarks, and lists marked up as lists.
  • Contrast: body text, small labels and button text meet at least 4.5:1; large text meets at least 3:1.
  • Reduced motion: scroll fades, the hero ease-out and the animated Private AI diagram all switch off when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Text alternatives: decorative icons are hidden from screen readers, and the Private AI diagram has a text description. Everything it shows is also written in the page.
  • Works without JavaScript: all content is in the page's HTML. If scripts fail to load, nothing stays hidden.
  • Forms: every field has a visible label, errors are announced to screen readers, and problems are described in text rather than by color alone.
  • Zoom and reflow: pages reflow to a single column at 320 pixels wide and support 200% zoom without sideways scrolling. Tables become stacked cards on small screens.
  • Touch targets: buttons and form controls are at least 44 pixels tall.

3. Known limitations

  • Labels inside the Private AI diagram are small on narrow phones. The same information is in the diagram's text description and in the surrounding text.

4. How we test

  • Keyboard-only walkthrough of every page and the contact form.
  • Automated checks for contrast, headings and labels.
  • Manual checks at 320 pixels wide, at 200% zoom, and with reduced motion turned on.
  • Screen reader spot checks with VoiceOver on macOS and iOS.

5. Tell us if we've missed something

If you run into a barrier on this site, we want to know. Email cwostrategygroup@gmail.com or call 614-965-4283 with the page address and what went wrong. We aim to reply within two business days and to fix confirmed barriers within 10 business days, or explain why it will take longer.

6. Accessibility on client work

Every website we build is developed to the same standard. Accessibility is included in the base price, not sold as an upgrade: it's cheaper to build in than to retrofit, and it's the right thing to do.
